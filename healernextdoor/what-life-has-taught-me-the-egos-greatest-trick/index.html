What Life Has Taught Me: The Ego’s Greatest Trick

Healing & Spiritual GrowthSeptember 21, 2026By Lisa Batitto

One of the most profound messages I’ve ever received from my guides was: “Enlightenment is the death of the ego.”

When I first heard those words, I wasn’t entirely sure what they meant. Like many people, I assumed enlightenment was something you achieved. I pictured someone who had reached a higher level of awareness and somehow stood apart from everyone else.

Over the years, that understanding completely changed.

People connect the ego with arrogance but it’s much more subtle than that. The ego wants to be special. It wants to be the most intuitive, the most gifted, or the most enlightened person in the room. It has a way of turning even spirituality into something we can compare, compete over, and use to define ourselves.

I now believe enlightenment asks us to let go of that very need.

The deeper I’ve gone on my own path, the less interested I’ve become in standing out. I don’t need to be the wisest person in the room, and I certainly don’t need people to see me as enlightened. What matters to me now is being present, listening with an open heart, and remembering that every person I meet has something to teach me.

Maybe that’s what my guides meant. Enlightenment doesn’t place us above other people. It reminds us that we were never separate from them in the first place.
